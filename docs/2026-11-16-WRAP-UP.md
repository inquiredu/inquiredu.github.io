# Creative portfolio wrap up for November 16

The portfolio is published at [inquiredu.org](https://inquiredu.org/), with editing through [the private studio](https://inquiredu.org/admin/). Today’s final design keeps the analog character while using the screen more efficiently. The final feature commit is [`ddbbc0c`](https://github.com/inquiredu/inquiredu.github.io/commit/ddbbc0c5fbad567b95e6679ee15e1213f325a72f), and its [GitHub Pages deployment](https://github.com/inquiredu/inquiredu.github.io/actions/runs/44004170788) succeeded.

## The design we settled on

**Photographs:** the fuller projector returns with an uncropped image, image-derived glow, round slide tray, and thumbnail strip. Hide controls folds the strip and controls away together, leaving the image and caption visible. The contact sheet, notes, Places artwork, and kaleidoscope remain available. Photos stay inline without a focus mode.

**Listening:** the existing cassette deck remains the main player. On wider screens, a compact stack of tape spines sits beside it. Selecting a spine reveals its clear case; Open case · Tracks swings the lid around a visible hinge. The reverse of the lid holds a paper insert with numbered titles, credits, durations where supplied, and internal scrolling. The cassette remains in the lower tray. Closing or stowing a case preserves playback; opening it never starts playback by itself.

SoundCloud originals retain handwritten labels. Spotify playlists use a mixtape treatment, and albums use a release treatment. The original music player can be expanded beneath the case. Focus view and the adjustable ambient bars/wave display remain available.

**Overall layout:** reduced section spacing, a bounded photo module, a shorter project terminal, and responsive side-by-side arrangements bring more of the collection onto the screen. The detailed music deck is about a third shorter in the tested desktop layout. Controls rearrange at smaller widths rather than scaling the whole interface down.

**Television and writing:** the chosen YouTube lineup retains its tube-TV enclosure, channel controls, table of contents, and focus view. The official player remains unobstructed. Medium and locally written pieces remain part of the collection when published; empty sections stay out of the way.

## Studio capabilities retained

GitHub owner login, repository selection, README-supported Studio help, the iPad-compatible AI connection, editable photo descriptions, and reviewable approximate place suggestions remain in place. AI suggestions require review and explicit publication. The public site stays open to visitors.

The backend and site are separate deployments. This closing design release changed the public presentation and supporting track-list data; it did not require another login grant or a Worker deployment.

## Current limits and future choices

| Area | Current state |
| --- | --- |
| Spotify printed insert | A dated snapshot of the 100 tracks exposed by the preview on 2026-11-16. It does not automatically synchronize or guarantee the complete playlist. The original player remains current. |
| SoundCloud printed insert | Loads the tracks exposed by its official widget. Studio-written track lists take precedence. |
| Music visualization | Ambient animation follows playback state; it does not analyze Spotify or SoundCloud audio. |
| Photo libraries | Selected-photo import from Apple Photos or files; no continuous iCloud or Google Photos library synchronization. |
| Recognized places | Approximate suggestions for owner review, not proof of camera position. Unaccepted suggestions stay private. |
| Studio help | Reads the selected project’s permitted README and GitHub details. It does not browse arbitrary linked sites or watch videos. |

Optional future work is automatic Spotify insert refresh, richer owner-managed track credits, and more convenient selected-photo sharing. These are separate choices, not unfinished requirements for the published release.
