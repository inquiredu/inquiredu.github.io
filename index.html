I'm an English teacher who builds things. I've spent twenty-five years in Minnesota public schools, from teaching language arts at the Hopkins Alternative Learning Center to directing technology for Orono Public Schools. Along the way I keep making things for the people in schools: guides, workshops, kits, and small apps that take unnecessary friction out of the work so their own talent comes through. I learn by building, so most of these started as questions. This is the catalog.
